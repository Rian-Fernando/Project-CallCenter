and direct extensions are deliberately excluded: they change often, and a stale
personal number is worse than a main line that always reaches someone.

## Which department to call

- **Trash, recycling, bulk pickup, missed collection** — Municipal Service Yard, 516-465-4031
- **Potholes, street lights, trees, snow, storm drains, sidewalks** — Public Works, 516-465-4003
- **Building permits, inspections, fences, sheds, decks, zoning** — Building Department, 516-465-4040
- **Property taxes and water bills** — Finance, 516-465-4166 (Tax Billing 516-465-4156, Water Billing 516-465-4160)
- **Parking permits, licenses, records, general questions** — Business/Clerk's Office, 516-465-4000
- **Parks, pool, tennis, camps, senior programs** — Recreation & Parks, 516-465-4075
- **Parking tickets and court matters** — Justice Court, 516-465-4080

## Water or sewer emergencies

Leaks, turn-offs, and sewer backups:
- **7:30 a.m. to midnight** — 516-465-4031
- **After midnight** — Village Police, 516-465-4100

## Landscaping, gardening and yard maintenance

- **Village parks, public greens and street trees** are maintained by
  **Recreation & Parks** (516-465-4075) and **Public Works** (516-465-4003).
- **Grass clippings, leaves, branches and yard waste** from a private property
  are a sanitation matter — Municipal Service Yard, 516-465-4031.
  Grass clippings are collected on the same day as garbage; branches go out on
  the Wednesday rubbish day.
- **Landscaper licensing** — 516-465-4052. Landscapers are responsible for
  removing and disposing of their own material.
- Private garden maintenance at a residence is the homeowner's responsibility;
  the Village does not perform it.
