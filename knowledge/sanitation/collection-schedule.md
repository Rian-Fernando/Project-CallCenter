# Garbage, Rubbish and Recycling Collection Schedule

Key facts extracted from the Village's official Sanitation/Recycling Collection
Schedule booklet and the Village Sanitation page.

**Why this file exists:** the source PDF is an 8-page booklet whose collection
table sits inside a printed calendar grid. Once split for search, that page
becomes mostly stray digits and weekday initials, and the one sentence a
resident actually needs stops being findable. This restates those facts in
plain prose so they retrieve correctly. Nothing here is added or inferred —
every line comes from the official documents.

**Booklet version note:** the file published at the URL above is the
**2024–2025** edition, even though the link is labeled 2026-2027. Holiday dates
below are therefore historical. Collection days should be confirmed against the
current booklet before a resident relies on them.

## Garbage and rubbish are collected on DIFFERENT days

This is the most common point of confusion, and the two must never be stated as
the same day.

- **Garbage** (household kitchen waste) — **twice a week**, on days that depend
  on the property's address.
- **Rubbish** (branches, cardboard, rags, books) — **every Wednesday**, the same
  day everywhere in the Village.

## When is garbage picked up? When is my trash day?

Household garbage is collected **twice a week**. The days depend on which side of
**Rockaway Avenue** the property is on:

- **Western Half** — west of Rockaway Avenue: **Mondays and Thursdays**
- **Eastern Half** — east of Rockaway Avenue: **Tuesdays and Fridays**

Because the day depends on the address, the correct response to a resident who
has not said where they live is to **ask which side of Rockaway Avenue they are
on**, not to give one day or the other.

Holiday weeks shift these days. The booklet lists the adjusted schedule for each
holiday.

## When is rubbish picked up?

**All rubbish is collected at the curb each Wednesday**, except as noted on the
Holiday Schedule. This is separate from garbage collection and applies to the
whole Village.

Rubbish includes tree branches (cut and tied in bundles), cardboard, rags, and
books. Grass clippings are collected on the same day as garbage, not on the
rubbish day.

## When to put it out

- Put material at the curb **by 6:00 a.m. on the day of collection**
- **Not before 7:00 p.m.** the evening before

## Garden City Recycling Center hours — what time is it open?

The Garden City Recycling Center, also called the Municipal Service Yard or
Transfer Station, is at 2 Cherry Valley Avenue.

- **Weekdays, Monday to Friday:** open 9:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.
- **Saturdays:** open 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.
- **Closed every day** between 11:45 a.m. and 12:30 p.m.
- **Phone:** 516-465-4031

## Hazardous materials

The Village does not collect hazardous materials. These go to the **Town of
Hempstead S.T.O.P. Program**, phone **(516) 378-4210**.

Not accepted in regular collection: aerosol cans that are not empty, propane
tanks, solvents and acids, chemicals, fertilizers and herbicides, paint,
gasoline and other flammable liquids.

The Village does **not** collect concrete or tires, and does not collect rubbish
from building construction, alterations, or repairs performed by a contractor.

## Items requiring a scheduled pickup

Some items are not part of routine collection and must be arranged in advance:
large mirrors, rugs, and carpets are listed as bulk items requiring a call to
schedule. Incandescent and LED bulbs also require a call.
