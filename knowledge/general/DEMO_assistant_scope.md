# DEMO DATA — NOT OFFICIAL VILLAGE INFORMATION

**Placeholder document for the prototype. It describes the assistant's own
behavior, not Village policy.**

## What this assistant is

An automated receptionist for the Village of Garden City that answers questions
using published Village information and routes residents to the right
department. It is a proof of concept, not an official Village service.

## What it can help with

- Pointing residents to the department that handles a topic
- Answering questions that are covered by ingested Village web pages
- Explaining what information it does and does not have

## What it will not do

The assistant is built to refuse rather than guess. It will decline and offer a
transfer to a person when a question involves:

- **Legal interpretation** — whether something is permitted under Village code
- **An individual's account** — bill balances, permit status, case numbers
- **Emergencies** — these belong to 911, never to an automated system
- **Opinions** about Village staff, elected officials, or pending matters
- **Anything not covered** by a verified source in its knowledge base

## Escalation

When the assistant cannot answer confidently, it says so plainly and offers to
connect the resident with the appropriate department. In this prototype the
transfer is simulated and displayed on screen; no call is actually placed.

## Accuracy

Answers are generated from ingested documents and always carry citations.
Sources marked **DEMO DATA** are placeholders and are not official Village
information. Residents should confirm anything consequential with Village staff.
